Zeon is a generative system that leverages the dynamics of your interactions with an LLM to enrich the dialogue and uncover, through unsupervised learning, new avenues for improving, extending, or rethinking the design and implementation of your technologies, or any other activity assisted by an LLM.

The installation is instantaneous: simply copy the engendering script from the file and paste it into your LLM’s prompt at the start of the session. In just 15 seconds, you’re ready to begin
